    One receiver. You choose the speakers.

    Advertise a single YouTube Music receiver and expose its audio as one Sendspin input. Speaker discovery and automatic routing are disabled in this mode.

    1. In MA, select your player or group.
    2. Open Browse → Sendspin Source and play the cast input.
    3. Cast from YouTube Music. You can also cast first, then select the input in MA.

    Playback waits until MA requests the input. Volume in YouTube Music adjusts the input audio. MA controls speaker volume, routing, and power. Stop Casting leaves your manual routing selected with silence.

    Seeking

    Keeping the output running delivers new-position audio much faster, but may feel more sluggish because old audio continues to play from MA and speaker buffers. Turn this on to interrupt old audio before seeking. Pause still stops output promptly.

    The current song is fetched ahead automatically while playback starts (32 MiB cache limit per receiver). This switch applies to per-speaker receivers; single-source mode leaves MA outputs alone.

    Local-only management · Switching modes, renaming, or disabling a receiver ends its casting session. Your per-speaker settings and receiver identities are preserved.
